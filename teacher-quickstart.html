5 Three flows you'll use every week

Run a live class: Live Teacher Mode → tick the online students → Activate. Their phones join your sentence builder automatically. Use 🖼 Describe image and 🧠 Sentence practice from the same screen.
Run a mock exam: HSK Simulation → pick the level and Sim → launch to selected students (they join in ~20 s) → watch live → check HSK Results after. Wrong answers appear in each kid's "Mis exámenes" with audio replays.
Homework happens by itself: at home, HSK1 kids train in 🎯 Entrenamiento de Simulación (3 tiers × 8 topics, real exam pictures & audio) and HSK2 kids play 🎒 Bolsa de Palabras (sentence-building). Parents track everything under 👨‍👩‍👧 Papás — including monthly report cards.
💡 Golden rule: your teacher code opens doors, the classroom code fills the room, and the student's own 4-letter code is their identity. Three codes, three jobs.
