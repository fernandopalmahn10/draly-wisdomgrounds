NEW EXP2 VOCABULARY DRILLED:
  lǎoshī    — maestro/a (used as TITLE after name: "Dralingo lǎoshī")
  xuésheng  — estudiante
  shū       — libro / pergamino
  dú        — leer
  kàn       — mirar
  zì        — carácter chino
  xiě       — escribir
  tīng      — escuchar
  shuō      — hablar
  Hànyǔ     — chino mandarín
  huì       — saber / poder (aprendido)
  xuéxí     — estudiar / aprender

GRAMMAR NOTE — title placement:
  Chinese uses SURNAME + TITLE (not "Teacher Smith" but "Smith Teacher").
  So "Dralingo lǎoshī" = "El maestro Dralingo" — title COMES AFTER the name.
  This is the correct, polite form.

GRAMMAR NOTE — possessive 'de':
  "Wǒ shì Dralingo lǎoshī de xuésheng" = "I am El maestro Dralingo's student"
  Pattern: [POSSESSOR] + de + [POSSESSED]
